RamAltman Windows · Effective November 9, 2026

RamAltman Windows is a personal, optional memory-reporting application. You control the Windows PC, sender account, recipient, and whether automatic reporting is enabled. Its source is derived from IainKay/ram-altman under the Unlicense.

What the application does

While enabled, the scheduled monitor checks available system RAM every five minutes while your Windows account is logged on. When available RAM meets the configured threshold, which defaults to at least 10%, it can send at most one report per local calendar day. Optional supported GPU memory readings are included separately.

Available memory is a capacity snapshot. It does not measure elapsed idle time, reserve or allocate your RAM, sell memory, or give a recipient access to your computer.

Your account and recipient

Authorize only an account you control and configure a recipient for whom you intend these messages. Use the tool on your own PC, respect email-provider rules, and stop if the recipient asks. You are responsible for the sender, recipient, and settings you choose.

The Gmail integration uses Google's own browser consent and API. Google's account, API, and email-service terms apply to those services. The project is independent of Google and OpenAI and does not represent the upstream author or any email recipient.

Control and privacy

Use Preview.cmd to inspect a report without sending it. Use Disable.cmd to turn the monitor off and Enable.cmd when you want it to resume. Disabling preserves settings and history; the privacy policy explains revocation and local deletion.

Availability and limitations

The tool depends on your PC being available, the scheduled task running under the intended Windows user, valid account authorization, and working network and email services. It cannot guarantee that a submitted email reaches a recipient's inbox. An uncertain delivery attempt blocks further sends for that day to avoid duplicate mail.
